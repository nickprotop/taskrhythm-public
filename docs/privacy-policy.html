TaskRhythm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

TaskRhythm (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal task and recurrence management application for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and your choices. We designed TaskRhythm to keep your data on your device by default; cloud sync is optional and only active if you create an account and sign in.

1. Data the app stores on your device

By default, everything you create in TaskRhythm is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted anywhere. This includes:

  • Tasks, notes, due dates, reminders, and priorities
  • Categories, tags, and subtasks (checklists)
  • Recurrence rules and completion history
  • App settings and preferences

This local data is never collected by us unless you enable account-based cloud sync (see below).

2. Account and cloud sync (optional)

If you choose to create an account to sync your data across devices, we collect and process the following:

  • Email address — used to create and identify your account, to sign you in, and to send password-reset messages.
  • Password — stored only as a salted, hashed value on our server; we never store or have access to your plaintext password.
  • Your task data — when sync is on, the task content listed in Section 1 is transmitted to and stored on our sync server so it can be delivered to your other signed-in devices.
  • Purchase token — if you buy the optional Pro unlock, the Google Play purchase token is stored on our server to verify your entitlement. We do not receive or store your payment-card details; payment is handled entirely by Google Play.

Your synced data is associated with your account and is used solely to provide the sync feature. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. What we do not collect

  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs are included in the app.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, or other device content.
  • No tracking of your activity across other apps or websites.

4. How your data is protected

  • Network communication with the sync server uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted storage on your device.
  • Passwords are hashed on the server using a strong, salted algorithm.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. If you use cloud sync, your synced data is retained on our server while your account is active.

You can permanently delete your account and all associated server-side data at any time, directly in the app: Settings → Sync & Account → Delete account. This asks you to confirm your password and then erases your account and all of its synced data from our server immediately and irreversibly. Your local on-device data is kept; you can remove that by deleting items in the app, clearing app storage, or uninstalling.

If you are unable to use the in-app option, you may also request deletion by contacting us at the email below.

6. Children’s privacy

TaskRhythm is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
